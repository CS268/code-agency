Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

What exactly does "done-with-you" mean?▼
Unlike "done-for-you" (we do everything for you) or "DIY" (you figure it out), done-with-you means we build TOGETHER. I guide you, I create the automations, but you learn to maintain them. Result: you're self-sufficient at the end.
I'm not technical, is that a problem?▼
Absolutely not! 90% of my clients have no technical skills. That's exactly why I handle the technical side. You just need to use the tools we set up.
How long before I see results?▼
From the first call, you walk away with 2-3 actionable quick wins. The first automations are deployed within 2 weeks. Average ROI is 5-10 hours saved per month from the 2nd month.
What tools do you use?▼
Mainly: Claude AI (skills/prompts), Make.com (automations), Notion (client hub), Voxer (async communication), Vercel (deployment). Depending on your needs: Stripe, Gmail, Airtable, Calendly.
Can I cancel anytime?▼
Yes, monthly commitment with no minimum duration. 30-day notice. Everything created belongs to you (skills, workflows, templates).
What type of business is this for?▼
Hairdressers, bakers, restaurants, business hosts, freelancers, small businesses. Any local business wasting time on repetitive tasks: emails, bookings, invoices, follow-ups, social media.
How is this different from a regular freelancer?▼
A freelancer handles a one-off task. AI Concierge is a continuous partner who deeply understands your business, anticipates your needs, and builds a complete automation system that evolves with you.
What happens during the discovery call?▼
30 free minutes: we discuss your business, pain points, and goals. I already give you 2-3 automation ideas. If it's a good fit, we schedule the first Audit call. Zero pressure.
Is my data safe?▼
Yes. GDPR compliant. Your data stays on European servers. No sharing with third parties. NDA available on request. You own everything that's created.
Are there hidden fees?▼
No. The monthly price includes everything. The only possible external costs: Make.com subscriptions (~€9/month) and Notion (free for most). I always warn you before any additional cost.
