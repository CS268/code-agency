Which plan to choose for your first video campaign on a tight budget?

November 5, 2026 • 4 min read

Why start with video advertising when you have a tight budget?

A retailer, brand, or independent professional looking to test video advertising without committing to a large volume may feel discouraged by traditional costs. Yet today, accessible solutions exist that allow you to generate impactful videos without technical skills or a significant budget. JCODE’s Studio Marketing offers three monthly subscriptions tailored to different needs, including the Discovery plan at 9.90 EUR per month. This plan lets you create up to five advertising videos per month, with various formats (9:16, 1:1, 16:9) and automatic integration of hooks and calls to action.

Imagine an independent professional wanting to promote a new product or a local service. With this plan, they can generate multiple videos without delay, without investing in expensive equipment or hiring an agency. The product remains faithful to the provided photo, ensuring a realistic and professional presentation.

Discovery: the ideal subscription to test video advertising

The Discovery subscription is designed for those who want to explore video advertising without committing to a large volume. For 9.90 EUR per month, it offers five advertising videos per month, with ten predefined campaign types (product, real estate, hospitality, etc.). The available formats align with the most widely used platforms: TikTok, Reels, and Instagram for 9:16, social media and newsletters for 1:1, and websites for 16:9.

This plan also includes a hook and call to action automatically integrated into the video, simplifying the creation process. No need to write scripts or hire an editor: everything is generated automatically from the provided visuals. The customer can focus on the essentials: selecting the right images and launching generation.

Pro: more videos, more effects, more flexibility

While the Discovery plan is suitable for a first test, the Pro plan at 24.90 EUR per month targets those who want to go further. With fifteen advertising videos per month and twelve additional marketing effects (luxury, neon, cinematic, etc.), this plan allows for more elaborate and impactful campaigns. The multi-visual queue makes managing several projects at once easier, while priority support ensures responsive assistance when needed.

This plan is particularly well-suited for independent professionals or small structures that need to regularly produce video content without overshooting their budget. The marketing effects add a professional touch, ideal for standing out on social media or in advertising campaigns.

Agency: for professionals managing multiple brands or clients

The Agency plan at 59.90 EUR per month is aimed at professionals who need to generate a large volume of advertising videos. With forty videos per month and everything included in the Pro plan, this formula is ideal for managing multiple brands or clients simultaneously. The high volume is perfect for large-scale campaigns where consistency and content diversity are essential.

This plan also includes dedicated support, making it a suitable choice for agencies or freelancers looking to outsource part of their video production. The customer can focus on strategy and creation, while the videos are generated by the tool.

How to launch a video campaign without hassle?

To start a video campaign with Studio Marketing, simply provide the visuals of the product, portrait, or location you want to promote. The customer can then enter a hook and call to action or let the tool integrate them automatically. The available formats (9:16, 1:1, 16:9) adapt to the target platforms, and the 720P quality is optimized for TikTok, Reels, and Instagram, while the 1080P quality offers sharper details for websites.

The process is simple: select the plan that fits your needs, provide the visuals, and start generation. The videos are ready to use without delay or required technical skills. Once the videos are generated, all that’s left is to publish them on the chosen platforms and analyze performance.

Conclusion

Choosing the right plan for your first video campaign depends primarily on your needs and budget. The Discovery plan at 9.90 EUR per month is perfect for testing video advertising risk-free, while the Pro and Agency plans offer more flexibility and volume for more ambitious projects. Whichever plan you choose, Studio Marketing enables you to generate powerful advertising videos tailored to modern platforms and ready for use without delay.
