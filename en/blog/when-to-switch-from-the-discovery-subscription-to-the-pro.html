When to switch from the Discovery subscription to the Pro subscription for a growing campaign?

November 3, 2026 • 3 min read

A video campaign that starts performing: the time to scale up

For a brand, launching a video campaign often begins with a testing phase. The goal is to validate the effectiveness of visuals and messages before investing further. The Studio Marketing’s Discovery subscription allows generating up to 5 video ads per month, with formats suitable for TikTok, Instagram Reels or other social platforms. This formula is ideal for exploring various creative angles without long-term commitment.

But when the first videos yield positive feedback — more engagement, increased clicks, more shares — the question naturally arises: how to ramp up production without compromising quality? Switching to the Pro subscription then becomes a logical step.

Discovery: the subscription to test and refine your strategy

The Discovery subscription offers 5 video ads per month, with 10 campaign types (product, real estate, restaurant, etc.) and formats 9:16, 1:1, and 16:9. Each video is generated from the visuals provided by the client, without distortion or artificial effects. The hook and call-to-action are integrated directly into the video, simplifying posting on social networks.

This formula allows comparing the performance of formats and creative angles. A retailer can identify which format converts best before committing to a more comprehensive plan. The subscription is cancelable at any time, reducing financial risks.

Pro: the subscription for large-scale production and marketing effects

The Pro subscription provides 15 video ads per month, three times more than the Discovery subscription. It also includes 12 marketing effects (luxury, neon, cinematic, etc.) to give videos style. The multi-visual queue enables launching several campaigns in parallel, ideal for testing multiple products or services simultaneously.

Priority support ensures responsive assistance when needed, valuable for brands looking to adjust their campaigns. With Pro, content production becomes more flexible and tailored to the needs of a growing campaign.

When to switch from Discovery to Pro? The signs that leave no doubt

Several indicators suggest it’s time to upgrade to the Pro subscription:

  • Demand exceeds supply: if the 5 monthly videos of Discovery are no longer enough to meet creative needs, Pro offers 15 videos to keep pace.
  • Need for creative variety: if the marketing effects of Discovery are insufficient to differentiate videos, Pro provides 12 additional effects to enrich the style.
  • Desire to test multiple products: with Pro, you can launch multiple campaigns at once, ideal for a brand wanting to diversify its offerings.
  • Importance of support: if assistance is needed, Pro offers priority support to resolve queries.

A solution designed for brands that want to evolve

The Studio Marketing targets brands, retailers, and independents looking to automate their video content production without sacrificing quality. A retailer or brand seeing its video campaign start to perform has every reason to switch to the Pro subscription. With 15 videos per month, 12 marketing effects, and priority support, this plan enables ramping up production while maintaining consistent quality.
