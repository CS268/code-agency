Why a content creator chooses to generate a video from a mix rather than a single source

October 23, 2026 • 3 min read

A demanding audience: agencies balancing precision and dynamism

Communication agencies and content creation studios seek tools that allow them to combine creative control with smooth visual flow. For them, a vertical video in 9:16 format must blend the sharpness of selected shots with the richness of natural movement. A mix of reference images and videos meets this need: it offers the ability to carefully choose each frame while incorporating a video sequence for fluid motion. This approach avoids the limitations of a single source, whether static images or raw footage that is difficult to segment.

This audience values flexibility. They do not want to be confined to one type of content but instead leverage the strengths of each medium. Images allow for capturing precise details, while video brings movement and visual continuity. By combining both, agencies achieve a balanced result tailored to the demands of platforms like TikTok or Instagram Reels, where visual quality and impact are decisive.

The prompt studio: a tool to structure your vision before generation

Before launching video creation, the prompt studio provides a key step: building a structured prompt. This consists of three essential elements: a long version, a short version, and a negative prompt. The goal is to guide AI tools such as Sora 2, Runway Gen-4, or Kling 2.0 toward a result that aligns with the creator’s vision.

The studio offers a selection of 260 cinematic effects across 23 categories. Each effect can be adapted to different formats: 16:9, 9:16, 1:1, or 2.39:1. Target durations for the prompt are 5, 10, 15, or 30 seconds, providing flexibility to adjust the final output. Once the prompt is finalized, it can be copied or exported as a .txt file, ready for use in the generation tool.

From prompt to vertical video: a seamless and uninterrupted process

After preparing the prompt, the process continues on the VIDEOCREATOR service. The user selects the type of reference: images only, video only, or a mix of both. For a mix, it is sufficient to add at least one image and one video to the same project. The service then transforms these elements into a vertical video, in 9:16 format at 720P.

Generation starts after the selection. A progress bar appears on screen. No duration is guaranteed, as it depends on the load on the AI tools used. If generation fails, the customer is not charged. Technical limits are clear: 15 MB per image, 50 MB per video, and a total of 50 MB for the entire project.

Conclusion: explore the prompt studio to refine your creation

A mix of reference images and videos gives agencies and creators the ability to produce vertical videos tailored to social platforms without compromising on quality or fluidity. Upfront, the prompt studio provides the means to structure your vision and prepare a ready-to-use prompt. Once the project is launched, the VIDEOCREATOR service handles the rest by converting the chosen references into a 12-second vertical video.
