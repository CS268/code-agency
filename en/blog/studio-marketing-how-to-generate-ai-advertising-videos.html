Studio Marketing: how to generate AI advertising videos without technical skills

October 15, 2026 • 2 min read

For merchants, independents, and small brands, creating effective advertising videos can be a challenge. The Studio Marketing by JCODE provides a solution by transforming your existing visuals into ready-to-use advertising videos, without manual editing or technical skills.

A tool designed for non-technicians

Studio Marketing automatically generates advertising videos from your product photos, portraits, locations, or dishes. You provide a hook for the first three seconds and a call-to-action (CTA), and the tool produces a video tailored to your campaign.

Available formats cover social media needs:

  • 9:16 (vertical)
  • 1:1 (square)
  • 16:9 (horizontal)

Three subscriptions to fit every need

Studio Marketing offers three monthly subscriptions, no commitment and cancelable at any time:

  • Discovery at €9.90/month: 5 advertising videos per month, 10 campaign types (product, real estate, restaurant, etc.), formats 9:16, 1:1, and 16:9, integrated hook and CTA, product faithful to the photo.
  • Pro at €24.90/month (most popular): 15 advertising videos per month, everything included from Discovery, 12 marketing effects (luxury, neon, cinematic, etc.), multi-visual queue, priority support.
  • Agency at €59.90/month: 40 advertising videos per month, everything included from Pro, multi-brand and multi-client, suitable for large-scale campaigns, dedicated support.

A result true to your image

Studio Marketing keeps the product faithful to the original photo, without distortion. Each visual you provide becomes a distinct advertising video, ready to be shared across your channels.

For whom?

This service is aimed at merchants, independents, small brands, and agencies looking to test advertising campaigns without technical skills or complex tools. The monthly subscription lets you get started and adjust the volume according to your needs.
