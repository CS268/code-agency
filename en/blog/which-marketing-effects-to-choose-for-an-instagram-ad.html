Which marketing effects to choose for an Instagram ad campaign?

October 28, 2026 • 2 min read

Why marketing effects turn your visuals into powerful ads

A single visual isn’t enough to grab attention on Instagram. Marketing effects add a professional touch that highlights your product or service without complex editing. They help create a consistent visual identity tailored to your target audience and industry.

These effects are applied directly to your existing photos or videos. They don’t distort the product but enhance it through lighting, colors, or textures. The result: an ad that stands out and drives action, even for creators or merchants without design experience.

Three subscriptions for different needs

The choice of subscription depends on your content volume and ambitions. The Discovery plan at 9.90 EUR per month is ideal for small businesses or independents looking to test ad generation. You get 5 ad videos per month, access to 10 campaign types, and all three formats (9:16, 1:1, 16:9).

For a more regular presence, the Pro plan at 24.90 EUR per month is the most popular. It lets you produce up to 15 ad videos per month, with 12 additional marketing effects (luxury, neon, cinematic, etc.). A multi-visual queue optimizes production.

Agencies or brands with multiple clients often choose the Agency plan at 59.90 EUR per month. This plan generates up to 40 ad videos per month, with dedicated support and the ability to manage multiple brands at once. Ideal for large-scale campaigns or frequent visual rotations.

Integrating marketing effects into your Instagram strategy

Start by defining a strong hook for the first three seconds of your ad. This hook sets the tone and immediately grabs attention. Add a clear call-to-action (e.g., "Discover our offers", "Book now") to guide the user to the next step.

Next, choose a marketing effect that fits your sector. For example, a "luxury" effect for a high-end restaurant or a "neon" effect for a night shop. The 9:16 format is optimized for Reels and TikTok, while 16:9 works better for stories or classic posts.

Test different combinations of visuals and effects to see what resonates best with your audience. The studio generates each ad within moments, allowing you to iterate and adjust your strategy based on performance.

Ads that respect your visual identity

The advantage of marketing effects lies in their ability to adapt to your brand image. They don’t create an artificial style but strengthen what you already have: your logo’s color, your photos’ style, or your establishment’s ambiance.
