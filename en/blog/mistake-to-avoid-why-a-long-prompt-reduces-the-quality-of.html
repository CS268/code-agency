"Mistake to avoid: why a long prompt reduces the quality of your vertical video"

November 10, 2026 • 2 min read

Why creative studios rely on concise prompts

An audiovisual creative studio aiming to produce vertical videos for TikTok, Instagram Reels, or advertising campaigns must rely on precise prompts. Yet, the temptation to detail every visual element in the prompt can harm the final quality. Tools like Sora 2, Runway Gen-4, or Kling 2.0 perform better when the text remains structured and concise. A prompt that is too long slows down processing, increases the risk of errors, and reduces the clarity of the generated image.

Teams that follow this rule achieve vertical 9:16 videos that stay true to their original intent. They avoid distortions, artifacts, or unwanted elements that appear when the model has to interpret an overly dense text. Conciseness also allows for testing multiple variations without adjustments.

The prompt studio: a tool designed to prevent excess

The prompt studio at https://jcode.store/studio/prompts/ offers 260 cinematic effects across 23 categories. It guides users by providing three distinct fields: a long version, a short version, and a negative prompt. This structure prevents stacking instructions into a single block of text.

This approach minimizes the risk of information overload and makes it easier to reuse parameters for other projects.
