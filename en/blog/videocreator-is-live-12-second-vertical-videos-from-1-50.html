VIDEOCREATOR is live: 12-second vertical videos, from €1.50

6 October 2026 • 2 min read

VIDEOCREATOR transforms your images or video into a 12-second vertical video in TikTok format. You pay online, from €1.50.

What it does

You upload your images (or a video), you describe what you want to see, and the studio generates a 9:16 video in 720P, lasting 12 seconds. It is the format for social networks watched on a phone.

We deliberately kept things simple: one duration, one format, three plans. No endless settings.

Three plans, a fixed price

  • Reference images: 1 to 8 images, €1.50
  • Reference video: one video, €2
  • Mix: at least one image and one video, €2.50

The price is displayed before payment. No subscription, no credits to buy in advance: you pay for the video you request.

How it works

1. You build your prompt in the JCODE prompt studio. It offers 260 cinematic effects, across 23 categories, and assembles them into a structured prompt.

2. You copy that prompt.

3. You go to video.jcode.store, choose your plan, upload your files (15 MB max per image, 50 MB per video, 50 MB total) and paste the prompt.

4. You tick the consent box and pay on a secure Stripe page. If the generation fails, you are not charged.

5. Your video is ready: you watch it and download it.

Who is it for?

For brands, creatives, studios, agencies and content creators: a teaser, an ad, an animated visual for social networks. It is not cinema. It is mainly a simple way to see what an idea looks like before going further.
